Who we are

A studio obsessed with people, ideas & possibilities.

GD+ is the communications & events division of Gulf Dunes — a small, sharp team in Dubai that believes the best brand moments happen when strategy, design and live experience are built by the same hands.

Our story

Founded on a simple idea: design should move people, not just pixels.

GD+ started as the events arm of Gulf Dunes and grew into a full communications studio because our clients kept asking for the same thing after every activation — "can you also tell this story everywhere else?" So we built the team to do exactly that: one roof for strategy, content, and the live experiences that bring it all to life.

Today we work with brands across the Gulf and beyond, from ambitious challengers to household names, on everything from a single campaign film to a four-day, multi-city launch tour.

What drives us

Explore. Create. Transcend.

Not a slogan on a wall — the actual shape of how a project moves through the studio.

01

Explore — we enable the pursuit of knowledge.

Before anything gets designed, we go looking: culture, category, competitors, and the very specific audience we're trying to move. Curiosity is step one, not a nice-to-have.

02

Create — we get creative with content.

Strategists, designers, producers and writers sit in the same room from day one, so ideas don't lose momentum crossing departments that don't exist here.

03

Transcend — we're innovative with knowledge.

We close every project with a hard look at what actually happened, then bring that knowledge into the next brief. Nothing we learn stays in a report nobody opens.

How we behave

Four things we won't compromise on.

Curiosity

Ask one more question than feels necessary.

The best brief is never the whole story. We dig until we find the bit nobody wrote down.

Craft

Sweat the details no one will ever mention.

Because the ones who notice are usually the ones deciding whether to hire us again.

Candor

Say the honest thing, early.

A hard truth in week one beats a nice surprise in week six, every single time.

Good chaos

Leave room for the happy accident.

Some of our favourite work started as a scribble that wasn't on anyone's timeline.
